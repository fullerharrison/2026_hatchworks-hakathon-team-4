The measured configuration was Moonshine Voice 0.1.5, English `TINY_STREAMING`,
`quantized_26_08_21`, CPython 3.12.11 on the developer Windows CPU. Exact model
artifact sizes/hashes remain in [runtime provenance](moonshine-runtime.json).
The three matched SDK floors were 0.5, 1.0 and 2.0 seconds with 100 ms ingress;
no unsupported CPU-thread flags were invented.

At the matched 0.5-second control, native Stop-to-final p95 was 1.815 seconds,
first live partial p95 1.414 seconds, gap p95 2.960 seconds, and median RTF 1.454.
Increasing the floor reduced median processing cost but delayed first/final
results; at 2.0 seconds every speech replay had just one live partial. Every
median native RTF remained above one. Whole-utterance p95 was 3.617-4.720 seconds
across the matched configurations, not a passing 3-second fallback.

Raw-reference WER was 25.00% whole utterance and 26.32% native in all three
matched configurations. Independent fixture diagnostics were numeric 6/7,
units 7/7, domain terms 9/11 and scoped negation 0/1 in both modes. Number-word
versus digit rendering is distinct from errors: "fumonisin" was misrecognized,
and "not seventy" became "Not seventeen." Verbatim transcripts and event
timelines are preserved; none was silently corrected.

All accuracy evidence is from 12 speech fixtures and one synthetic voice.
Repetitions are timing observations, not additional independent accuracy samples.
The 180 tuning silence trials produced empty text. There were zero submitted
actions. No held-out breeder, noise, browser-render or live-microphone validation
was performed.
