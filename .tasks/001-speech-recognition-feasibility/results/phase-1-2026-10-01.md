## Parakeet Interim Findings

The pinned protocol body was obtained through its raw public URL after the
webpage extractor returned no meaningful content. The pinned recognizer's
`preview` calls its transcription path, corroborating redecoding rather than
cached incremental inference.

- Protocol 2 partials are opt-in, advisory and replace the previous running text.
  Sequences increase within a session; unchanged text is omitted. Final
  `complete` text is authoritative, and failure/loss counters must be inspected.
- Documented cadence is approximately 500 ms of captured audio from v0.1.9,
  with at least 400 ms pending; these are implementation settings, not measured
  end-to-end first-partial latency.
- Uncommitted tails are re-encoded and capped at the newest 15 seconds; a
  `truncated` partial may omit earlier uncommitted speech. Preview decodes add
  300 ms silence. Costs quoted by upstream are Apple Silicon-specific.
- `transcribe --session --partials` replays files through the session pipeline;
  it is not evidence of a physical microphone or remote browser integration.
- v0.1.10 `keep_warm` retains an open microphone and buffers inter-session audio.
  Do not enable it in the proposed explicit Start/Stop-only minimum slice.
- Queues skip busy previews instead of accumulating them. Final loss accounting
  does not count skipped previews; cadence/churn must be measured separately.

## Workflow And Data Contract

The current [API reference](../../../app/API.md) was reread. Ask accepts
candidate/revision context; filter interpretation and validation produce
reviewable proposals, not applied state. Existing preference behavior and
unsupported write/policy intent remain as scoped by the [task](../task.md).
No actual workflow replay or browser/local-storage inspection was executed here.

Required proposed contract: partials are display-only; final text is editable;
the person explicitly chooses Ask, filter proposal or a selected form field;
existing validation and human confirmations remain authoritative. Browser-only
preferences need a separately approved adapter. Scoring-policy edits stay out of
scope. The existing LLM path may transmit final text and context externally;
local ASR alone does not make the complete workflow offline.

Browser microphone capture requires a secure context: localhost may qualify,
but a plain-HTTP LAN demo address is not equivalent. Actual origin, microphone
permission, worker/WASM threading and mobile behavior are untested. No UI was
created and no microphone permission was requested.
